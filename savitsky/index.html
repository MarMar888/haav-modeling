Model scope and sources

Calm-water force and pitch equilibrium for a constant-deadrise prismatic monohull; Savitsky 1964 wetted lengths, ITTC 1957 friction and Townsin roughness allowance. The solver searches 0.5–35° trim and flags trim outside 2–15°, wetted-length ratio above 4, beam speed coefficient above 13, dry chines and wetting beyond hull length. Speeds with a beam coefficient below 1 are not evaluated.

Passing these checks does not establish that the boat will plane or be stable. No porpoising, wave response, appendage drag, air drag, propulsion efficiency, electrical power, endurance or CAD import is included. Effective power is hull resistance × speed, not battery power. Static trim and draft are not calculated.
