# Welcome to Haav

Haav stands for Highly Amphibious / Autonomous Vessel. We are a student team at UW-Madison building an autonomous boat from the ground up, with every decision modeled before it is built.

<Figure src="hull.jpg" alt="CAD rendering of the Haav hull, a hybrid planing hull" caption="Our current hull design." />

## The goal

A planing hull carrying a 30 pound added payload over a 2 mile course. We are planning to enter the PEP2027 Autonomous Uncrewed Division, a 2 mile point to point time challenge.

## What is next

- Oct 25: hull design frozen
- Nov 1: preliminary design review
- Nov 10: critical and long-lead parts ordered
